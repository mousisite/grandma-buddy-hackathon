# Rose's story (the opening, 20 to 25 seconds)

Rose is made up. She speaks in the first person, warm and plain, in her own voice (Kokoro, `video/intro/rose-story.wav`). No self-pity, no medical claims: she names small everyday troubles and ends on the one thing she wanted.

Both versions below hit the same beats in the same order: she lives alone, her eyes, the confusing phone, the lost glasses, the pills she can't remember, the strange calls and letters, the family she misses, and the closing line.

## Version A: plain (48 words)

> I'm Rose. I live on my own. My eyes aren't what they were, and the phone is a puzzle. I lose my glasses. I forget if I took my pills. Strange calls, and letters I don't understand. I miss my family. I just wanted someone kind to talk to.

## Version B: in her voice (52 words). Use this one.

> My name is Rose. It's quiet in the house now. I can't see so well, and the phone confuses me, so I leave it alone. My glasses wander off. Did I take my pills? I'm never sure. Odd calls, odd letters. I miss my daughter. I just wanted someone kind to talk to.

Why B: the short lines ("My glasses wander off." "Did I take my pills? I'm never sure.") sound like a person remembering, not a list, and "I miss my daughter" sets up Maria, who appears later on the family page. A is the fallback if B runs long against the scenes.

## The transition (the cut to the app)

Rose, over the last scene, as the app fades in (`video/intro/rose-transition.wav`):

> Then my daughter set up Buddy.

## Timing

In Rose's voice (Kokoro's "Kore", about 155 Hz where Buddy's "Heart" is about 209, at speed 0.8 where Buddy speaks at 0.9) version B runs 22.5 seconds and version A 20.9; `scripts\video_voice.py` prints the lengths when it runs. Six scenes under 22.5 seconds is about 3.8 seconds each, with the portrait in a corner.
