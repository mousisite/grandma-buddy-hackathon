                  How Buddy works
                  
        
                  Buddy is an AI helper made with four of Meta’s AI models. Everything else runs here, on this computer.
                  
        
                    
        
                      
        
                      
      
                      

      
                      
        
                      What Buddy never does
                      
        
                        
          
                      • Give medical advice. Your doctor is the right person for that.
                        • 
          
                      • Call 911 by itself. It shows a big button, and you decide.
                        • 
          
                      • Send your words to anyone unless you press Send.
                        • 
          
                      • Ask for passwords, bank numbers, or your Social Security number.
                        • 
          
                      • Pretend to be a person. Buddy is an AI helper.
                        • 
        
                      
      
                      

      
                      
        
                      Your privacy, in short
                      
        
                        
          
                      • What Buddy writes down (your diary, pills, and Memory book) is kept on this computer.
                        • 
          
                      • What you say is sent to Meta’s AI so Buddy can understand and answer. Buddy uses Meta’s standard models only, so Meta doesn’t use your words to train its AI.
                        • 
          
                      • Photos you show Buddy to check a message, read a letter, or find your things are never saved.
                        • 
          
                      • Your family sees only the update you choose to send them.
                        • 
        
                      
      
                      

      
                      
        
                      Questions
                      
        
                      
          
                      
            Is Buddy a real person?
            
                      No. Buddy is an AI helper. It is kind, and it remembers what you share about your life, but it is not a person. The people who care about you are always the ones to call.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            Can Buddy tell me what to do about my health?
            
                      No. Buddy never gives medical advice. It writes down how you feel, in your own words, for your doctor, and it can help you call your doctor or your family.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            What happens in an emergency?
            
                      If you tell Buddy about a warning sign, like chest pain or a fall, it shows a big Call 911 button right away, before any AI is asked. Buddy never calls by itself: you decide.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            Can Buddy read my mail to me?
            
                      Yes. Tap “Read it to me” on Home and take a photo of a letter, a bill, or a form. Buddy says what it is and what it says, or reads it word for word. A letter about your health is only read to you, never explained: your doctor is the right person to ask about it.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            Does Buddy listen all the time?
            
                      No. Buddy listens only while the talk screen is open, and never while it is talking itself. It stops when you tap Pause or leave the talk screen, and after a minute of quiet.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            Who can see my diary?
            
                      It is kept on this computer. Your doctor sees it on the notes you print or bring, and your family sees only the update you choose to send.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            How do I make the words bigger, or the voice slower?
            
                      Open Settings. Text size has three sizes, and Voice speed has Slower.
                      
          
                      
          
                      
            Does Buddy work without the internet?
            
                      Your pill reminders and the safety net work on this computer, even without the internet. Talking with Buddy needs the internet.
                      
          
                      
        
                      
      
                      

      
                      
        
                      Still need help?
                      
        
                      Your family is happy to help. In an emergency, call 911.
